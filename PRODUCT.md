# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Cash-on-delivery shoppers in Tanger, Morocco. Most customers arrive on mobile, while desktop shoppers need a familiar, information-rich ecommerce layout for browsing, comparing, and completing an order with confidence.

## Product Purpose

TanjaMall helps customers discover products, understand the delivered price and COD process, and place a short guest order. Success means the full flow from published product to confirmed WhatsApp order is clear, fast, and trustworthy on both mobile and desktop.

## Brand Personality

Practical, warm, confident. The storefront should feel local and approachable while maintaining the clarity and polish of a serious modern retailer.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a stretched phone mockup, a generic SaaS landing page, a crowded marketplace, or a luxury editorial site. Avoid decorative complexity, excessive animation, account-first flows, online-payment UI, and unsupported trust claims.

## Design Principles

1. Make the product and its price the visual priority.
2. Explain COD, Tanger delivery, and confirmation at the moment each detail matters.
3. Preserve the approved compact mobile rhythm while giving desktop its own information architecture.
4. Use familiar ecommerce patterns so customers never have to learn the interface.
5. Keep every public surface focused on completing a real order.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast and keyboard access. Maintain clear focus states, semantic landmarks, descriptive labels, reduced-motion compatibility, and minimum 44px touch targets on mobile.
